## Featured project

### [AI Search Visibility Auditor](https://github.com/kawakicodes/ai-search-visibility-auditor)

Shows whether a website can be found on Google and in AI assistants like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Claude, and what to fix if it can't.

<a href="https://github.com/kawakicodes/ai-search-visibility-auditor"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kawakicodes/ai-search-visibility-auditor/main/docs/overview.png" alt="The auditor's results screen: five websites with Google and AI-search scores and their top issues" width="720"></a>

- Paste in a list of websites and get two scores out of 100 for each, with every problem explained and a "what to do" next to it
- Spots hidden blocks, such as a website's security settings quietly stopping ChatGPT or Perplexity from reading it
- Ranks the websites by how much help they need and writes a ready-to-send pitch email for each one
- Re-checks your client websites every morning and can email you only when something breaks or gets worse

**Results**
- **Caught 2 client websites silently blocking Perplexity's AI crawler** within the first week of monitoring 16 client sites, by building a daily monitor that knocks on the door as each AI search crawler instead of only reading the site's rules
- **Cut a full visibility check of a client portfolio to one command**, covering 16 websites across 26 scored checks each, by building a tool that scores Google and AI-search readiness and explains what to fix
- **Closed every critical and high defect from 2 independent review rounds**, locked in by 49 automated tests, by testing it as a first-time customer, a senior developer and an accuracy checker

Free to run on your own computer, built in Python with AI coding assistance.

## More projects

### [Snippet Checker](https://github.com/kawakicodes/snippet-checker) · [try it live](https://kawakicodes.github.io/snippet-checker/#demo)

See how page titles and descriptions will look in Google before you publish them. Drop in a spreadsheet; it measures every title in pixels, previews it like Google, and flags anything cut off, missing or duplicated.

- **Raised a 494-page law-firm client site from an average score of 62 to 98**, cutting likely cut-off titles from 115 to 1 and missing descriptions from 494 to 0, by checking every rewritten title and description with this tool before delivery
- **Checks 3,000 pages in under a second**, entirely in the browser, so client data never leaves the computer
